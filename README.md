## 🧠 How Prediction Markets Work (in a Nutshell)

At its core, a prediction market is an exchange where you can buy and sell "shares" in the outcome of future events. The price of these shares reflects the crowd's collective belief about the probability of that event occurring.

### 1. Create or Join a Market

*   **Create:** Propose a new market for an event you're interested in (e.g., "Will Bitcoin reach $100k by end of 2025?").
*   **Join:** Browse existing markets and find events you want to predict on.

### 2. Buy Shares (Stake YES or NO)

When you participate in a market, you buy shares in either a "YES" outcome or a "NO" outcome.

*   **Example:** If you believe Bitcoin *will* reach $100k, you buy "YES" shares. If you believe it *won't*, you buy "NO" shares.
*   **Share Price & Odds:** The price you pay for each share is determined by the current market odds.
    *   If the market believes there's a 75% chance of "YES", then "YES" shares might cost $0.75, and "NO" shares $0.25.
    *   The more people buy "YES" shares, the higher their price goes, and the lower the price of "NO" shares (and vice-versa).

### 3. Real-time Odds & Projected Payouts

Qflow Cloud provides real-time updates on market odds. As you consider staking, you'll see:

*   **Current Odds:** The live probability of each outcome.
*   **Shares to Acquire:** How many shares you'll get for your stake amount.
*   **Projected Payout:** Your potential earnings if your predicted outcome occurs.

### 4. Manage Your Bets: Early Exit (Sell Shares)

Unlike traditional betting, Qflow Cloud allows you to manage your exposure. You can "sell" your shares back to the market at any time before the market resolves.

*   **Dynamic Payouts:** The amount you receive when selling shares depends on the *current* market odds. If the odds have moved in your favor since you bought, you might sell for a profit. If they've moved against you, you might incur a loss.
*   **Small Fee:** A small transaction fee is applied to early exits.

### 5. Market Resolution & Final Payouts

Once the event occurs and the market's end date is reached, the market "resolves" to either "YES" or "NO".

*   **Winning Shares:** If you hold shares in the winning outcome, each share will be worth $1.00.
*   **Losing Shares:** Shares in the losing outcome become worthless.
*   **Full Payout:** Your final payout is calculated based on the number of winning shares you hold.
